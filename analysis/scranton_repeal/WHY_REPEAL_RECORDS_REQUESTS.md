# Records requests: why the split rate was repealed

Drafted 2026-10-24 for `WHY_REPEAL.md`. **None has been sent.** Each fills a gap the public web
does not cover: who proposed the single rate and on what analysis, the officials' own financial
interests, and their donors. Status is logged in `rtk_tracker.csv`. Add the requester's name and
address at sending; keep the as-sent copy in `rtk_outbox/` (git-ignored, because it carries that
address), as for requests 1 and 2 in `RTKL_REQUEST_DRAFT.md`.

---

## Request 3: City of Scranton, Statements of Financial Interests

**To:** City of Scranton Open Records Officer, 340 N. Washington Ave, Scranton, PA 18503 ·
rtk@scrantonpa.gov · 570-348-4100 ext. 9409. (Custodian of the forms: City Clerk Frank
Voldenberg, same address.)

**Records requested** (Right-to-Know Law, 65 P.S. § 67.101 et seq.; the forms are public records
under the Public Official and Employee Ethics Act, 65 Pa.C.S. § 1104(e)):

The Statements of Financial Interests filed with the City for **calendar years 2023, 2024 and
2025** (filed in 2024, 2025 and 2026) by:

- Mayor Paige G. Cognetti;
- Council members Gerald Smurl, Mark McAndrew, Jessica Rothchild, Thomas Schuster and William
  King;
- Business Administrator Eileen Cipriani, and the Deputy Business Administrator / Finance
  Director;
- the City Controller.

Electronic copies (PDF scans) by email are preferred.

*Why:* Section 1105(b) requires each filer to list any office or employment in a business
entity (item 8), **any financial interest in a for-profit legal entity** (item 9), sources of
income of $1,300 or more (item 5), creditors owed more than $6,500 other than a home mortgage
(item 4), and real estate only where it was sold or leased to or from a government or condemned
(item 3; statutory thresholds, which the Ethics Commission may adjust). The form does **not**
list an official's real estate in general. Item 9 is the one that matters here: it would name
an LLC that holds land-heavy parcels, which a name search of the roll cannot connect to an
official.

## Request 4: City of Scranton, records on the change to a single millage

**To:** as Request 3.

**Records requested:**

1. Any memorandum, analysis, spreadsheet, presentation or report prepared by or for the City
   (including by the Pennsylvania Economy League, PFM, or any other consultant) between
   **2025-01-01 and 2025-12-31** that discusses replacing the City's separate land and building
   millage rates with a single millage rate, or that estimates the effect on any class of
   property of keeping separate rates after the countywide reassessment.
2. Emails sent or received between **2025-07-01 and 2025-12-16** by the Mayor, the Business
   Administrator, the Deputy Business Administrator / Finance Director or the City Solicitor,
   to or from any City Council member, Council's solicitor, the Lackawanna County Assessor's
   Office, or any consultant named above, containing any of the terms "split rate",
   "split-rate", "two-tier", "single millage", "single rate", "land tax" or "land millage".
3. Any record explaining why the draft 2026 millage ordinance placed on the 2025-11-25 Council
   agenda was withdrawn and replaced by File of the Council No. 107 of 2025.

*Why:* the budget narrative states the reasons but not their origin. Item 1 would show whether
anyone modeled who gains and who loses from a single rate on the new values before Council
voted; item 2 who proposed it; item 3 the only recorded change of course.

*Note:* the Commonwealth Court reads email requests for specificity on subject matter, time
frame and scope; each item above names all three. Expect the City to invoke the 30-day
extension, and to withhold drafts under the predecisional-deliberation exemption
(§ 708(b)(10)); factual analyses are not covered by that exemption and should be released.

**Records requested:** copies of every campaign finance report and its schedules (cover page,
contributions, expenditures, debts) filed **from 2023 through 2025** by the candidate
committees of:

- Paige Cognetti (Friends of Paige Cognetti);
- Gerald Smurl, Mark McAndrew, Jessica Rothchild, Thomas Schuster and William King;

including the 2025 pre-primary, post-primary, pre-election, post-election and annual reports,

*Why:* municipal candidates file with the County, not the Department of State, and the County

*Note:* the Election Code requires the filing officer to make these reports available for
public inspection and copying within two days of receipt and to keep them five years (25 P.S.
§ 3259, the supervisor's duties; cited from a secondary summary, so confirm the text before
quoting it in the letter). An ordinary
written request should be enough; send it under the Right-to-Know Law,
to Traci Harte (as for Request 1), only if the Department asks for one. Under the City's Code of
Ethics (§ 12(2)(B)(i)), candidates also file a duplicate with the City Clerk, a second source if
the County is slow.
